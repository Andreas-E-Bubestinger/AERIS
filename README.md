# AERIS

**AERIS (ÆRIS) — Independent AI Systems & Governance Research Project**

**Architect and Researcher:** Andreas Erwin Bubestinger  
**Location:** Austria  
**Project lineage:** documented since 2025

AERIS is an independent research and development project focused on artificial intelligence systems, governance, functional continuity, reproducibility, provenance, validation, agency, and responsible human–AI interaction.

The project investigates how model-external architectures can support persistent system context, controlled continuity, evidence-aware operation, governance boundaries, reproducible validation, and responsible interaction across changing AI models and runtime environments.

## Research Areas

Current research areas include:

- AI systems architecture
- functional identity continuity
- memory and provenance
- AI governance and bounded agency
- reproducibility and validation
- causal and ablation-based evaluation
- human–AI interaction
- model-independent orchestration
- embodiment and multimodal system integration

## Research Approach

AERIS follows an evidence-oriented engineering approach.

Development claims are separated from validated results, and technical capabilities are evaluated through controlled testing, reproducibility checks, versioned artefacts, cryptographic hashes, and explicit claim boundaries.

Where appropriate, internal architecture components are tested through controlled comparison, ablation, placebo, replay, and reconstruction procedures.

## Public Research

Selected research results, methodological work, publications, and provenance records are made public where doing so does not disclose protected implementation details.

The complete AERIS runtime architecture, source code, internal module structure, operational parameters, routing logic, prompts, state operationalisation, and other implementation-specific material are not published in this repository.

This repository serves primarily as the public identity, research, publication, and provenance reference for the AERIS project.
